About AUTO MOTION

AUTO MOTION — automatic video editing.

Turn your iPhone photos and videos into a ready-to-share vertical clip — without manual cutting, endless rearranging, or trying to hit every beat by eye.

Choose your photos and videos, add music — Auto Motion analyzes the track and automatically edits your footage to its rhythm.

The app detects the tempo, musical accents, and changes in energy, then finds the best moments in your original videos and arranges your clips so the visuals move with the music.

This is more than a slideshow or a random cut. Auto Motion looks for the most expressive moments in your videos, determines where cuts work best, and turns your footage into a cohesive video that’s ready to share.

Music changes the edit

Changed the track? You don’t have to start over. Auto Motion can rebuild the edit around the new music — adjusting clip lengths, cut points, and the overall pace to match the new track.

  • Calm music — smoother editing.
  • An energetic track — more movement and more frequent cuts.
  • A strong beat — cuts aim to land on musical accents.

You control the result

Automatic editing is only the beginning. After generation, open the editor and fine-tune the moments you want: trim clips, change their order, replace footage, adjust transitions, and more.

Rhythm

Stick to the beat of the music.

MOTION listens to the track, maps the hits, and uses them as a grid. Smooth, Energetic, or Cinematic styles change how long shots linger and how sharp the moves feel. BPM and rhythm offset stay in your hands if the analysis needs a nudge.

Depth

3D Photo with volume.

A still does not have to sit still. MOTION separates the subject, adds parallax, and lets you ride Depth and Camera until the scene feels like it has air around it. Pair it with music and the subject can move on the beat.

Privacy

Full processing right on your iPhone.
