Access note

Core editing, recipes, and local projects are available on iPhone with iOS 16 or later. Some advanced looks and extra project room may use an App Store subscription presented inside MOTION. After a trial or subscription ends, free limits apply again to new work. Your media remains on the device either way.

Rhythm

Stick to the beat of the music.

MOTION listens to the track, maps the hits, and uses them as a grid. Smooth, Energetic, or Cinematic styles change how long shots linger and how sharp the moves feel. BPM and rhythm offset stay in your hands if the analysis needs a nudge.

Depth

3D Photo with volume.

A still does not have to sit still. MOTION separates the subject, adds parallax, and lets you ride Depth and Camera until the scene feels like it has air around it. Pair it with music and the subject can move on the beat.

Privacy

Full processing right on your iPhone.

No cloud render queue. No waiting for someone else’s server to finish a cut. Photos, camera clips, voiceovers, and exports stay on the device unless you choose to share them. Read how that works on the Privacy page.
