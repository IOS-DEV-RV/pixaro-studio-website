Privacy Policy

Vintage Camera · Effective Date: July 27, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected or processed in the Vintage Camera mobile application (the "Application"), available on iPhone. By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. What is Vintage Camera

Vintage Camera is an iPhone camera app that helps you capture photos and videos with the timeless look of film photography and Polaroid pictures. It combines familiar camera features with vintage styles, frames, effects, captions and optional place marks previewed in the viewfinder.

3. Data Required for Functionality

The Application may request access to the following system features:

  • Camera — used to capture photos and record videos with live film looks, frames and effects in the viewfinder.
  • Microphone — used when you record video with sound.
  • Photo Library — used to save captures you choose to keep, and to import photos when you select media from your library.
  • Location — used only when you enable place marks, so city and country can appear on the frame. Location is processed on your device for this feature and is not required for basic camera use.

You can manage these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Local Storage

Most Vintage Camera content stays on your device, including captured media you save, selected looks and frames, editor preferences, captions and local settings. This helps keep your work available inside the Application without requiring an account.

5. Analytics

We use Mixpanel to understand feature usage, improve product stability and detect technical issues. Mixpanel receives event-level analytics data that does not directly identify you. Learn more at mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Subscription Services

7. Payments

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription renewal and cancellation are managed in iOS Settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only with service providers required for Application functionality, analytics, diagnostics or when legally required.

9. Security

10. Children
