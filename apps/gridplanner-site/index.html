Key features

Everything you need to plan a feed that looks intentional.

▦

Grid Planning

Add photos from the camera or library, reorder posts, duplicate them and build the exact structure of your feed with a clear, editable layout.

◎

Profile-Style Preview

Switch to Preview to see the grid with profile chrome: avatar, name, username and counters. Customize Profile Preview in Settings to match how you want it to look.

⇄

Plan, Preview & Drafts

Move freely between planning, preview and drafts. Save unfinished ideas and return when the layout is ready.

⧉

Multiple Plans

Create separate plans for different ideas and accounts, switch between them, duplicate strong versions and archive plans you don’t need right now.

◫

Compare Plans

Compare two plans side by side or in an overlay stack to pick the more balanced layout before you publish.

⤓

Import Existing Grid

Upload a screenshot of your current profile, crop to the posts area and keep planning from there. Reorder imported groups and reuse previous imports later.

☰

Carousel Posts

Create scrolling posts with up to 20 photos in one place — ideal for multi-frame stories inside your grid.

✎

Built-in Photo Editing

Crop, straighten, rotate and flip photos, adjust light and color, apply filters, and choose formats for posts, Stories, 4:5 and more. Replace photos anytime without losing the plan.

✦

Overlays

Add overlays on photos and refine the composition directly in the editor.

▤

Post Details

Add captions and personal notes, choose a format, edit, replace, archive, restore or delete posts — all from a clear detail screen.

◷

Publish Reminders

Set local reminders for the date and time you choose, so you publish on schedule and don’t forget your plans.

▦

Archive & Restore

Move posts out of the active grid when you need space, then bring them back when the timing is right.

↗

Share & Export

Share your grid and export it as HEIC, JPEG or PNG with the quality that fits your workflow.

✓

Built for Simplicity

A clear path from first launch to a finished plan. Grid Planner is for people who care how the whole feed looks — not only single posts.
