Privacy Policy

Grid Planner · Effective Date: August 1, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected or processed in the Grid Planner mobile application (the "Application"), available on iPhone. By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. What is Grid Planner

Grid Planner is a visual feed planner that helps users arrange posts and carousels into grids, import screenshot layouts, edit photos, manage multiple plans, and schedule local publish reminders.

3. Data Required for Functionality

The Application may request access to the following system features:

  • Photo Library — used to import photos into posts, capture or import grid screenshots, and export finished images when you choose to save or share.
  • Camera — used when you choose to capture a new photo for a post or grid import.
  • Notifications — used for publish reminders you schedule yourself. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

You can manage these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Local Storage

Most Grid Planner content is stored locally on your device, including plans, posts, drafts, archive items, overlays, editor preferences and reminder schedules. This helps keep your work available inside the Application without requiring an account.

5. Analytics

We use Mixpanel to understand feature usage, improve product stability and detect technical issues. Mixpanel receives event-level analytics data that does not directly identify you. Learn more at mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Subscription Services

7. Payments

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription renewal and cancellation are managed in iOS Settings.

8. Data Sharing

9. Security

10. Children
